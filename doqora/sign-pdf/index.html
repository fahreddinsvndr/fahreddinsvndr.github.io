Sign a PDF on your phone in under a minute.

Contracts, rental forms, permission slips: open the PDF in Doqora, add your signature and send it back. Nothing is uploaded, and you do not need an account.

How it works

  1. Open the PDF in Doqora, from Files, email or any app's share menu.
  2. Tap Sign and draw your signature with a finger or stylus, or type it.
  3. Drag and resize the signature until it sits right on the line.
  4. Save, then share or print the signed PDF.

Questions

Can I reuse my signature?

Yes. Save it once and place it with a single tap next time.

Is the signature added to the PDF itself?

Yes. It is written into the PDF, so it shows in any PDF reader.

Is signing free?

Yes. Signing and form filling work without a subscription.
