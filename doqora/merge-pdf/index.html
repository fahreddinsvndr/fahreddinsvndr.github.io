Merge PDF files into one, on your phone.

Join scans, invoices and chapters into a single PDF, put the pages in the right order and share one file. Files are combined on your device, never uploaded.

Download on the App StoreGet it on Google Play

How it works

  1. Open the first PDF in Doqora and go to Pages.
  2. Tap Merge and pick the other PDFs to add.
  3. Drag pages into order, rotate or delete the ones you do not need.
  4. Save the merged PDF and share it.

Questions

Can I split a PDF too?

Yes. Select pages and save them as a new PDF.

Is there a limit on files?

Merging PDFs in the page editor is free. The batch tool, which merges many PDFs in one step, is part of Doqora Pro.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Merging happens entirely on your device.
