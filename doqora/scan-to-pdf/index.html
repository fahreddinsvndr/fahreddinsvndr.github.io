Scan paper documents to searchable PDFs.

Point your camera at receipts, contracts or notes. Doqora finds the edges, cleans up the page and saves a PDF, and on-device OCR makes the text searchable.

Download on the App StoreGet it on Google Play

How it works

  1. Tap the scan button on Doqora's home screen.
  2. Capture one or more pages; edges are detected automatically.
  3. Review the pages, add more or retake.
  4. Save as PDF with searchable text, then share or sign it.

Questions

Does OCR send my document to the cloud?

No. Text recognition runs on your device.

Can I add pages to an existing scan?

Yes. Keep scanning and the pages are appended.

Is scanning free?

Yes. Scanning and OCR work without a subscription.
