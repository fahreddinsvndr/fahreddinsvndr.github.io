What Doqora does

  • Fill and sign

    Type into PDF form fields, then draw or type your signature and place it anywhere on the page.

  • Annotate

    Highlight, underline, strike out, add notes and text boxes, or draw freehand in several colours.

  • Scan with OCR

    Turn paper into clean PDFs with your camera. On-device text recognition makes scans searchable.

  • Pages and files

    Reorder, rotate, delete, merge and split pages. Compress large PDFs and convert JPG to PDF and back.

  • Protect

    Password-protect PDFs with AES-256, permanently redact text, and lock documents with a PIN.

  • Private by design

    Files are processed on your device and never uploaded to our servers. No sign-up, no login.

Free and Pro

Doqora is free to download, and the core tools (reading, annotating, form filling, signing, scanning with OCR, merging and splitting) work without a subscription. Doqora Pro removes ads, adds batch tools and exports without the small Doqora watermark. Prices are shown in the app.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. There is no sign-up and no login.

Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

No. Doqora edits, signs and scans on your device. Your files are never sent to our servers.
