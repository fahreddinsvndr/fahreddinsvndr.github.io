Track every subscription. Know before it renews.

Recur is a calm subscription tracker and bill reminder. You add your subscriptions by hand, and Recur shows what they really cost each month and year, warns you before each renewal and points out the ones you have stopped using.

What Recur does

  • True monthly and yearly spend

    Totals are kept per currency on the amounts you entered. Recur never converts between currencies, so the numbers match your statements.

  • Renewal reminders

    An on-device reminder before each renewal, plus an optional Monday summary of the week ahead.

  • Unused subscriptions

    Mark how often you use each service. Insights shows what you pay for things you rarely open, so you know what to cancel.

  • Private by design

    No bank connection, no email scanning, no account. Your list stays on your phone.

  • Quick add

    Pick a popular service from the catalogue and fill in your own price and date.

  • 74 languages, light and dark

    The whole app is translated, in a light and a dark theme.

Free and Premium

The free version tracks up to 10 subscriptions with reminders and the full Insights tab, and shows ads. Premium adds unlimited subscriptions, no ads, cloud backup to your own iCloud or Google Drive, and JSON import and export. Prices are shown in the app.

Questions

Does Recur connect to my bank?

No. You add subscriptions by hand. Recur never asks for bank details or reads your email.

Is there a free subscription tracker plan?

Yes. Up to 10 subscriptions, reminders and Insights are free.

Can I track subscriptions in different currencies?

Yes. Each subscription keeps its own currency and totals are shown per currency.
