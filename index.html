Recur Subscription Tracker

Know what your subscriptions really cost.

Recur is a calm subscription tracker for iPhone and Android. You add what you pay for, it tells you the monthly and yearly total, and warns you before anything renews. No bank connection, no account — everything stays on your phone.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What it does

Your true monthly spend

Every subscription rolled into one monthly and yearly figure, so the small ones stop hiding.

Warnings before renewal

A reminder a few days ahead, at a time you choose — while cancelling is still easy.

Each currency on its own

Totals are grouped per currency and shown in the amount you are actually billed. Recur never invents an exchange rate.

What you have stopped using

Insights flags the subscriptions you marked as rarely used, so you can decide what to drop.

Yours, on your phone

No sign-up and no server. Your list lives in the app; cloud backup, if you turn it on, writes to your own iCloud or Google Drive.

20 languages, light and dark

Fully translated, with currency and language picked up from your device on first launch.
